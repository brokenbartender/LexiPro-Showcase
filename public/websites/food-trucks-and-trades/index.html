What has to be readable

Five facts a customer, and an assistant, can quote.

If any of these exist only inside a JPEG, a story, or a post that asks you to log in, they are missing from search and from AI answers.

01 · Hours

Plain-text hours

Today's open and close times, written as words. A photo of a chalkboard or a whiteboard does not count. If the truck moves, the week's stops belong in the same text.

02 · Menu or services

A list, not a screenshot

The menu, the service list, or the starting prices, as HTML. A price-sheet JPEG, a flyer, and a photo of the specials board stay as pictures. The facts also have to be sentences.

03 · Service area

Where you actually work

The cities you serve, the lot you park in, or the neighborhoods you will drive to. "DM for location" is not a service area a stranger or an assistant can use.

04 · Contact

A phone number or booking link

A direct phone number or a booking link in the page text. A Messenger button, and a number printed inside a photo, are not a contact path Google or an assistant can hand to someone.

05 · Schema

LocalBusiness JSON-LD

A LocalBusiness block in the page source: name, phone, hours, and service area, matching the visible text. It does not buy a ranking. It stops the machine from having to guess.
