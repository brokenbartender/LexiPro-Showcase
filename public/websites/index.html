Typical scenario

A food truck posts its weekly schedule as graphics inside Facebook: an image of a chalkboard on Monday, a story on Wednesday, a flyer on Friday. The truck's Google listing has no website, or it points to the Facebook page.

A customer asks ChatGPT "where is the taco truck this Saturday?" The assistant cannot read the schedule because it lives inside images on Facebook. It either guesses, says it does not know, or recommends a competitor whose schedule is published as text on a real website.

A one-page site turns that schedule into plain text with structured data. Google can index it. AI assistants can quote it. The customer finds the truck.
