What I fix & ship in 7 days

Proof, not guesses — applied to your listings.

Every deliverable targets a specific reason AI assistants skip, misquote, or fail to book a local business:

  • 01
    Unblocking AI crawlers. Your robots.txt and firewall settings checked and corrected so OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, and PerplexityBot can actually read your site.
    Day 1–2
  • 02
    Extracting trapped facts into plain text. Services, starting prices, consultation fees, and cancellation policies pulled out of PDFs, images, and pop-up calculators into server-rendered HTML the engines can quote.
    Day 2–4
  • 03
    Cross-platform fact reconciliation. Name, address, phone, hours, and service menus made identical across your website, Google Business Profile, Yelp, and the directories — so no source contradicts another.
